Simple tomato soup. Heat two tablespoons of oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add one chopped onion and cook until soft, about five minutes. Stir in four cups of chopped tomatoes, two cups of vegetable broth, a teaspoon of salt and half a teaspoon of pepper. Simmer for twenty minutes, then blend until smooth. Serve hot with fresh bread. Serves four. Keeps for three days in the refrigerator.
