Lemma 2. For every integer n greater than 1, the sum of the first n odd numbers equals n squared. Proof. By induction on n. For n = 2 we have 1 + 3 = 4. Assume the claim holds for n, then adding the next odd number 2n + 1 gives n squared plus 2n plus 1, which is (n + 1) squared. This completes the proof.
